A learning space
that moves with you.

CATION is a place to explore science, technology, engineering and mathematics by following your curiosity, not a fixed syllabus. Pick a discipline, find a subject, and go as deep as you like.

01

How CATION teaches

  1. 01

    Start with a question

    Every course opens with something worth wondering about, and every module ends by answering part of it.

  2. 02

    Learn by doing

    Labs let you change a variable and see what happens, so ideas are tested rather than memorised.

  3. 03

    Make the invisible visible

    Forces, fields, data and change are hard to see. CATION draws them, the way each discipline’s plate does.

  4. 04

    Be honest about progress

    Anything unfinished is labelled as unfinished. Nothing pretends to work before it does.
